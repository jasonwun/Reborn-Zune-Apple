# Scope tiers and explicit non-goals

Scope is tiered: **α (tvOS)** is the tile wall only — auto-shuffled bundled
music, tap a tile to skip — because the Siri Remote has no good browsing
interaction model. **β (macOS, iPadOS)** is the tile wall plus a Now Playing
strip and a folder picker on first launch; the tile wall stays the centerpiece.
**γ is deferred**: album/playlist UI, sort/filter, connected animations,
mini-player.

Explicit non-goals: Apple Music / MusicKit catalog integration, drag-and-drop
playlist editing, equalizer / DSP / visualizer, iCloud sync, App Store
distribution. The explicit no-s are as binding as the yes-s; revisit them
deliberately, not by drift.
